About TrainMe

Malaysia's dedicated platform bridging the gap between university students and meaningful internship opportunities.

Our Story

TrainMe was born from a simple frustration: Malaysian university students were drowning in job boards designed for experienced professionals. Every listing demanded "3-5 years experience," and real internship opportunities were buried under layers of irrelevant noise.

We created TrainMe to be the solution — a dedicated, noise-free ecosystem where every single listing is an internship, every company is verified, and every student gets a fair shot at launching their career.

Founded in 2025 by a team of former interns and HR professionals, TrainMe is built specifically for the Malaysian market, supporting students from UM, MMU, APU, UiTM, UTM, Taylor's, and beyond.
